Frequently Asked Questions

Why do I need a CPA who specializes in ranching?

Ranching has tax rules that don't exist anywhere else: farm income averaging, weather-related sale deferrals, raised livestock basis, breeding stock depreciation. A generalist will miss these—and it costs $20K–$60K per year, every year.

How much can I actually save?

Most ranches we work with save $20,000–$60,000 per year through proper tax planning. Larger operations with succession planning needs can save much more. We'll give you an honest estimate on your first call.

Can you really help with succession planning?

Yes. We've helped ranches worth over $50 million transfer between generations without losing land to taxes. It requires proper structure, early planning, and someone who understands both the tax code and the ranching business.

What's "farm income averaging"?

Section 1301 lets you average farm income over the previous 3 years to lower your tax bracket. If you had a big cattle sale this year, we might be able to save you $15,000+ by spreading the income backwards. Most CPAs don't even know it exists.

Do you work with smaller operations?

We work with operations from 200 head to 10,000+ head. The tax strategies apply regardless of size—though larger operations often have more complexity and more opportunity.

I'm not in New York. Can you still help?

Absolutely. We serve ranchers in all 50 states. Everything is virtual—we work with ranches from Montana to Texas to Florida. No need to be local.
